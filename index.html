Our Story

A Legacy of Flavour & Passion

Founded in 1998 by Chef Éric Laurent, Aurum was born from a singular vision: to create dishes that transcend the plate and become experiences. Our kitchen is where classical French technique meets seasonal provenance and modern imagination.

Every ingredient is sourced from trusted artisan producers across Europe. Our sommelier team maintains a cellar of over 800 labels. From the first amuse-bouche to the final petit four, every detail is considered.
